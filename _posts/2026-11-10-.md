Jesus is ever-loving, ever-giving, and ever-present. He's present in you, me, and in...Star Wars?! 

His love is so great, that he always tries to make himself known for us, even in places you wouldn't believe. He's in the church, the mall, and even on the big screen! 

Welcome to Reel Jesus, and here, I like to take some time to show the presence of Jesus in some of my favorite things. From the way a character acts, to the tone they speak, and the themes in the story, seeing these parallels has only made me love my favorite pieces of media more, and hopefully, it will help you love them too.

Knowing Jesus is within them has not only made me have a greater appreciate for these clear expressions of art, but given me solace in knowing The Lord is always looking after me. 

So, take some time my friends, as we dissect the presence of our everlasting savior everywhere! Amen and Alleluia!
